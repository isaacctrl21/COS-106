Academic Profile & Background

I am Isaac Adelagun, an aspiring cybersecurity professional currently enrolled as a 100 Level student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Cyber Security at Miva Open University.

This website serves as my practical submission for COS106: Introduction to Web Technologies. Through this course, I am building strong foundational knowledge in client-side internet architectures, web accessibility, semantic markup, responsive design principles, and DOM-driven scripting without reliance on third-party frameworks.
